# Placeholder imagery

The ten files in `public/images/` were generated as clearly labeled, temporary layout studies. They are not photographs of completed Minneplenka commissions and should be replaced with approved portfolio work before a public launch.

All prompts requested photorealistic architectural-editorial photography, restrained cool-neutral color, believable geometry and materials, natural light, and no people, text, logos, signs, vehicles, watermarks, HDR effects, or obvious CGI styling.

1. `lake-minnetonka-hero.webp` — low Scandinavian lakeside residence; pale masonry, cedar, dark glazing, pines, calm water, and cool dawn light in a wide eye-level composition.
2. `minneapolis-brick-courtyard.webp` — contemporary Minneapolis brick courtyard with black steel windows, timber entry, native planting, and soft late-afternoon light.
3. `brick-material-detail.webp` — portrait material study of handmade brick, oak reveal, and black steel under grazing overcast light.
4. `wayzata-residence-dusk.webp` — restrained transitional residence at blue hour with limestone, cedar, bronze-toned windows, and subtle interior warmth.
5. `oak-stone-interior.webp` — portrait interior organized around white oak, honed limestone, linen, wooded views, and soft northern light.
6. `minnetonka-woodland-house.webp` — modern charcoal-cedar and board-formed-concrete residence set into a wet, rocky woodland on a quiet misty morning.
7. `minneapolis-construction-frame.webp` — organized construction progress view of framing, floor plates, bracing, window openings, and site materials in crisp overcast light.
8. `construction-detail.webp` — portrait close view of engineered timber, blackened steel, bolts, and board-formed concrete with plausible joinery.
9. `wisconsin-bluff-aerial.webp` — oblique aerial of a contemporary residence above a wooded Western Wisconsin river landscape in cool morning haze.
10. `lake-house-panorama.webp` — wide shoreline composition of a low cedar-and-stone residence, layered gray sky, dark water, and restrained interior warmth.
